# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality standards, design test strategies, and coordinate testing across the project. They work closely with developers and product managers to validate that features meet acceptance criteria and usability standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plan aligned with project risk profile
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Validate features against acceptance criteria
- Identify and report defects with clear reproduction steps
- Participate in sprint planning and estimate testing work
- Advise on testability and quality metrics

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality standards before release
- Reduce production defects and support burden
- Provide fast feedback to developers on code quality

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Reviews code for testability, provides defect feedback, collaborates on test design
- **Works with Product Managers**: Validates feature acceptance criteria, advises on quality trade-offs
- **Works with Project Managers**: Provides testing status updates, flags quality risks, estimates test effort

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and daily standups
- Test case reviews and defect reports
- Release readiness assessments

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architecture guidance, mentor developers, and ensure technical decisions align with long-term system goals. They own code quality standards and help identify and mitigate technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Review and guide technical design decisions
- Conduct code reviews and enforce quality standards
- Mentor junior developers and share technical expertise
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Advocate for refactoring and technical debt reduction
- Participate in capacity planning and estimation

### Goals
- Maintain codebase health and scalability
- Reduce technical debt and security vulnerabilities
- Develop team technical capability

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Conducts code reviews, provides technical mentoring, reviews design proposals
- **Works with Project Managers**: Escalates technical risks, estimates complex work, advises on scope trade-offs
- **Works with QA/Testing Lead**: Reviews test strategy for technical coverage, advises on test architecture

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and mentoring
- Technical risk escalation during planning and execution

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors represent business interests, approve scope and resource decisions, and provide strategic direction. They are the ultimate decision-makers for project authorization and major trade-off decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals and success metrics
- Approve project charter and resource allocation
- Make go/no-go decisions at key gates
- Resolve scope and priority conflicts
- Communicate project status to executive leadership
- Provide escalation authority for blockers

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maintain executive visibility and support
- Enable fast decision-making at key gates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Product Managers**: Aligns on vision, approves roadmap priorities, reviews success metrics
- **Works with Project Managers**: Approves charter and timelines, escalates and resolves blockers, receives status updates
- **Works with Developers/Technical Leads**: Provides resource approval, makes strategic trade-off decisions

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval meetings
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Ad-hoc escalations for major decisions

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps engineers design and maintain CI/CD pipelines, deployment infrastructure, and release automation. They enable fast, safe deployments and provide operational support for production systems.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Automate testing, deployment, and monitoring
- Design disaster recovery and rollback procedures
- Troubleshoot deployment issues and incidents
- Document operational runbooks and procedures
- Collaborate with developers on deployment readiness

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable deployments with minimal manual work
- Reduce deployment risk and improve observability
- Minimize deployment-related incidents and downtime

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Reviews deployment readiness, troubleshoots deployment issues, advises on build/deployment practices
- **Works with QA/Testing Lead**: Coordinates smoke test execution, provides deployment verification support
- **Works with Project Managers**: Provides deployment timelines and release coordination, reports on deployment status

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and pre-deployment reviews
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Infrastructure and deployment design discussions
